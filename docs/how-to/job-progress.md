# Job Progress

Admins can open **Job Progress** by clicking **Admin** in the sidebar. The arrow beside Admin opens its submenu for Administration and other settings. The register shows one row for each job returned by the configured ServiceM8 connection. Search by job number, customer, or site address, and switch between all jobs in the period, review candidates, and upcoming bookings. The upcoming view shows jobs with a future booking, earliest booking first.

The default period is **7 days**. Choose **14 days**, **30 days**, or **All dates** to change which jobs appear. The periods use the ServiceM8 job record's **Last Update** edit date, measured back from the time shown above the register. Jobs with no valid edit date appear under All dates. Search and view choices stay in place when you change periods.

The register shows **5 jobs per page** by default. At the bottom, choose **10**, **20**, or **50** and click **Apply** to show more. The selected page size stays in place when searching or switching periods and views; those changes return to page one.

**Next Upcoming Booking** remains the earliest scheduled future booking for each shown job, regardless of the update period. “No Upcoming Booking” is shown only when the complete future schedule read succeeds.

The register does not include complete Job Diary history. **Last Update** is not the latest Diary activity, and Job Summary does not establish the actual next action or whether somebody is following up. Review candidates are active Quote or Work Order jobs without a future booking; they are a starting point for checking, not a complete attention list.
