# Moving through lists

Below a paginated list, the left side shows how many items match, the center shows **Previous**, the current page, and **Next**, and the right side has **Page size** and **Apply**. Choose a page size and click **Apply** to return to page one with that many rows. Available sizes and defaults may differ by list.

Searches and filters continue to work as before. Where a page has multiple lists, each list keeps its own page and size when you change the other.
